That was when Yahaba finally realised—Kunimi had probably been like this ever since the Inter-High Nationals two months ago.Both teams representing Miyagi had been eliminated in the semifinals—Aoba Johsai lost to Inarizaki, and Karasuno fell to Itachiyama.

Aoba Johsai's championship streak had been broken, and Miyagi had returned to that brutal state where only one team could advance to Nationals.

At the time, only Watari had been able to attend the matches in person. Yahaba was taking exams, while Taichi and Kyōtani were tied up with club scrimmages.

No one had been prepared to lose.Especially not Aoba Johsai, whose goal had always been to dominate the nation.

"You guys already did amazing," Yahaba said, giving Kunimi a pat on the shoulder. "Didn't we only make the top eight the first time we went to Nationals—"

"We'll take back the championship at Spring High," Kunimi cut him off calmly.

Yahaba paused. Kunimi's tone was utterly serious—not the slightest trace of bravado in it.

"Kunimi'sreallystrong now," Watari Shinji added with a smile. "He's Aoba Johsai's ace now."

To be fair, the current crop of high school players no longer had super-elite spikers like Kaedehara Taichi or Ushijima Wakatoshi. ButVolleyball Monthlystill selected the nation's top spikers each year.

Right now, the top three spikers in the country were: Aoba Johsai's Kunimi Akira, Inarizaki's Riseki Heisuke, and Fukurodani's Sarukui Fūto(ranking in no particular order).

If you extended the list to the top five, it also included the first-year ace of the reigning Inter High champions, Chiba Chidō, and Karasuno's ace from the Inter High Final Four, Kita Seio.

The one thing Yahaba felt genuinely grateful for was that Kyōtani Kentarō was in the same grade as him. Otherwise, their year at Aoba Johsai would've gone down as the one that produced absolutely no standout talent...

"Hmm?"

Kindaichi Yūtarō glanced left and right. Ever since Kunimi joined the conversation, the discussion had grown harder to follow.

But Kindaichi wasn't one to let a conversation die.He took a deep breath—

"Ladies and gentlemen! This afternoon's match is about to begin!"

"Hey, Kindaichi, what are you doing? Hurry up!" Yahaba called back.

Both teams played in V1 League, but the gap between elite clubs and ordinary ones ran deep.

Miya had been one of the first to learn that Taichi would go pro after his second year in college. He himself had already received an offer from Black Jackals around that time.

For just a moment—no,for more than just a moment—Miya had fantasised about winning the championship with Taichi on the same team.

Inarizaki and Aoba Johsai had practically become sworn rivals, but the cooperation during the Japan–USA exchange match had left Miya yearning for more.

When he found out that Black Jackals had extended an offer to Taichi as well, Miya had immediately called to ask for his answer.

"Ah, I'm not joining Black Jackals," Taichi had replied casually.

"Why not?" Miya had asked, confused. "You, me, Bokuto, Sakusa…we're the strongest of our generation.We'd dominate V1 for sure."

"Mm…"

"Our peak periods even line up. It wouldn't just be a team—it'd be a dynasty."

"Mm…"

"Forget EJP or AD—they wouldn't stand a chance!Wait,are you thinking of joining one of those two?"

"Nah, and they didn't invite me either."

"They didn't invite you?" Miya thought about it. It made sense—neither team needed to reinforce their outside hitter lineup.But AD and EJPhadsent offers to Miya, and that thought gave him a flicker of satisfaction.

"So whereareyou going? Tachibana Red Falcons? Green Rockets?"

"Mm…Hornets," Taichi had said.

"Huh?Hornets?" Miya had frowned. That was a name he didn't recognise. "Osamu, lend me your phone. I need to look up that club!"

"Don't you have your own?" Miya Osamu had called from another room.

"I'm on the phone with Taichi!"

"Catch!"

"Nothing—hold on." Miya had quickly searched up DESEO Hornets' information.

A minute later, he double-checked it just to make sure he wasn't mistaken.

"Taichi, why Hornets?" he asked calmly. "Because of Iizuna-senpai? Or do you know someone else on the team?"

"Nope," Taichi had answered.

"Then why not join Black Jackals? You heard everything I said just now, didn't you?" Miya's voice was tight with frustration.

"Tooboring."

"Huh?" Miya had frowned. "What's that supposed to mean?"

"I said, winning like that would be too boring," Taichi had replied airily.

Even over the phone, Atsumu could practically see Taichi's usual indifferent expression.

"Exactly because you guys are so strong…it's more interesting to face you as opponents,not teammates," Taichi had said with a laugh. "Don't you think?"

Miya Atsumu had fallen silent for a moment, then laughed too.

"Fair enough.In that case,next time we meet—I'll crush you."

"You're as dramatic as ever, Atsumu-senpai," Taichi's voice dropped. "I've said it before—it doesn't matter where I am.Any team I'm on will win."
