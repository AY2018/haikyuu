Ryujin's first match of the Club World Championship turned out to be far more gruelling than expected.

As usual, Kaedehara Taichi sat next to Chiba Kaho, offering his characteristic sarcastic commentary while watching the match. After Ryujin won the third set, they substituted Kunimi Akira out, and Taichi promptly lost interest in continuing to watch.

Of course, he never imagined the match would drag on all the way to a decisive fifth set.

Later, at Chiba Kaho's suggestion, the two decided to take a stroll around the arena.

"Don't think that just because of this, I'll forgive you," Chiba said, pouting as she ate the dessert Taichi had bought her.

"Y-Yeah…"

To be honest, Taichi wasn't entirely sure where their relationship stood. While dating during high school in Japan wasn't anything unusual, they hadn't quite gotten that far and had already begun walking separate paths.

Yet,after that one time they held hands, he finally realised the subtle change in their dynamic. But soon after, he graduated early and joined the DESEO Hornets in Aichi.

Until he achieved his goals,Taichi didn't have the bandwidth to think about anything else.

Their feelings for each other progressed slowly and cautiously. Chiba wasn't in a rush, gently guiding the pace forward. They often chatted on their phones, and over time, Taichi found that the total time he spent messaging Chiba had surpassed that of talking to Kenma or even all the Aoba Johsai members combined.

However, about a year and a half ago, Taichi's idiotic move completely infuriated Chiba—he left for Argentina without telling anyone(Kunimi only knew he was going abroad).

When Chiba received her acceptance letter to Nagoya University, Taichi was already in Argentina, blissfully thinking he had given everyone a"pleasant surprise."

"So, if you win the championship this time, will you come back to Japan?" Chiba asked, tilting her chin slightly.

"There's no'if.'Iwillwin, and then I'll come back," Taichi replied confidently, as if stating an undeniable fact.

"I see~"

Chiba let out a small laugh—this unshakable confidence was precisely what she liked about him.

But with that answer, her plan to go to Argentina was rendered meaningless.Deep down, she felt a tiny twinge of disappointment;after all, she had secretly imagined what it would be like to surprise Taichi by suddenly showing up in Argentina.

Taichi and Chiba didn't walk around for too long—about three hours at most. In the afternoon, there was an important match in Group A, and Taichi had to return to analyse their upcoming opponents with the San Juan team.

The two eventually wandered back to the venue. Iwaizumi Hajime gave Taichi a strange look as he took the big bag stuffed with souvenirs from him.

"They actually went to five sets?"

Taichi glanced up at the updated scoreboard above the arena.

With this result, Ryujin would have 2 points and Al-Rayyan 1 point. But in Group B's case, they probably wouldn't need to calculate points to determine advancement, Taichi thought idly.

"Oikawa already went back; you should hurry up too," Iwaizumi said.

"Oh, thank you, Iwaizumi-senpai."

After saying goodbye to Chiba, Taichi prepared to leave.

"Taichi."

Iwaizumi hesitated for a moment before calling out.

"I know it might sound strange coming from a Japanese trainer…but you and Oikawa have to win it all!"

"Of course!"

"Sorry I'm late."

Taichi returned to San Juan's resting area. On the court, both teams had already finished warming up and were about to begin.

"It's fine. We understand."

Ramos gave him a playful wink.

His teammates were all smiling knowingly, even Coach Jose had a"you rascal, hiding something like this the whole time"expression on his face.

"…"

Damn that Oikawa. What exactly had he said?Taichi could only hope Iwaizumi had given him a good scolding earlier.

"Hinata…how much have you changed since high school?"

Kageyama Tobio stood at the service line, the ball spinning slowly between his hands.

"Tweet!—"The whistle sounded.

The ball stopped spinning and was tossed high into the air.

"I can hear every sound around me clearly. Lunch has digested just right. My vision is perfect…"

Kageyama felt an unprecedented calm settle over him.

Kageyama took his approach, leaped high, his body stretching to its limit in midair. His right arm swung backwards, fully loaded with power, then whipped forward in a sharp, explosive motion.

"I'm nothing like who I was in high school anymore."

The ball shot high into the air—a first pass that was only half-accurate!

"Double block!" Alexander and Zaytsev jumped together.The attack route was sealed!

BOOM!!!—

The ball slipped right through the narrow gap in front of them. Luca Boro rushed forward —

SMACK!!!

A sharp, tight cross!

The ball skimmed Boro's arm and struck the court at a devilish angle, hitting the floor a meter inside Trentino's left-side three-meter line!

Yoandy scored!

[São Paulo 1 - 0 Trentino]

"I told you, there are others who can pull off a cross shot like that," Zaytsev said with a helpless smile.

"Since you received it…consider that point a gift from me."

Kageyama and Hinata exchanged a knowing look across the net.
