Chapter 558

"Yaku, has this guy always been this cocky?"

Taichi's words sounded threatening, but to them, it felt more like he was throwing a tantrum because he couldn't shake Nizhnev off.

Even with Taichi in the front row, the score merely oscillated back and forth without a big gap; now that he was in the back row—what could he possibly do?

Off the block and down!

Point for Taichi!

[San Juan 17 – 16 Nizhnev]

Loser's second serve!

"Bruno—!"

"——?!"

Nizhnev's block had no time to react.Who would have expected that, with one touch left, Taichi would boldly choose a full-on spike from beyond the baseline?!

"Ivan!"

Yaku shouted.

The ball came faster than anyone imagined. Ivan panicked, hurriedly lifting his arms.

Smack!!

The ball popped high but luckily stayed in play.

Yaku quickly adjusted and pushed the ball back to the net from beyond the baseline.

Viktor adjusted and attacked!

"Double block!" Ramos and Conte both jumped.

This time, they finally covered the ball with their block.

BOOM!!!

"One touch!"

Whoosh!—

The ball arrived right on time.

"Double block!" Kobzar and Viktor forced themselves up but were clearly a step too late.

—BOOM!!!

The ball smashed down at Nizhnev's three-meter line, bouncing high.

Point for Taichi!

[San Juan 18 – 16 Nizhnev]

Rather than being shocked by the reappearance of his "ultra quick attack," what Kobzar couldn't figure out was—how did Taichi even get there?!

San Juan pulled ahead by two points while Taichi was still in the back row…

"Tweet!—"

No time to recover. Loser's third serve flew out!

This time, it dropped between Ivan and Mikhail, avoiding Yaku.

Whoever took the ball would at least occupy one of Nizhnev's back-row attackers.

Smack!

Mikhail moved over and steadied the receive.

One of Nizhnev's biggest headaches now was their inability to score. Apart from trusting his spikers, Kobzar had no better option.

Whoosh!—

The ball flew to the middle. Viktor jumped again.

San Juan's front row was tightly marking Viktor and Igor, leaving Ramos alone to block Viktor.

But in the back row, Taichi, Bruno, and Santiago stood in a tight line, staring down Viktor's attack.

BOOM!!!

Viktor avoided the block and pounded it down hard.

Smack!

The ball flew to San Juan's right deep corner, bouncing off Santiago's arm and out of bounds.

Point for Viktor!

[San Juan 18 – 17 Nizhnev]

"Great spike!"

Kobzar shouted.

Viktor's thunderous spike lifted morale, finally halting San Juan's run.

Serve change. Nizhnev to serve. Mikhail rotated to the front, and Nizhnev entered their strongest rotation.

"Tweet!—"

Kobzar's jump serve thundered over!

Taichi watched the oncoming ball, a slight smirk at the corner of his lips.

Smack!

He took it easily, charging forward, adjusting his body as he ran.

Thud!

Taichi jumped from the back row!

Kobzar's serve hadn't hindered him in the slightest!

Oikawa quickly covered and jumped to set.

"Triple block!" Viktor, Igor, and Mikhail all jumped up.

Seeing Taichi so arrogantly attack again, they aimed to crush him outright!

Whoosh!—

"Hmm?"

BOOM!!!

The ball flew right to the right wing—Conte faced an open net and smashed it home!

Point for Conte!

[San Juan 19 – 17 Nizhnev]

"Damn it—!"

Igor roared, frustrated.

As a middle blocker, Ramos understood their frustration deeply.

When an attacker is constantly bouncing around in front of you, all you want is to block them into the floor.

In this entire match, they hadn't shut down Taichi even once. They didn't want to miss such a "golden chance"…

Unfortunately, that mindset was perfectly manipulated by their vile setter.

"All of you crowding in front of Taichi really gave me a shock~"

Oikawa faked a surprised face, but the smirk at his lips was infuriating.

Serve change. San Juan to serve. Taichi rotated to the front row.

"Tweet!—"

Conte, riding the momentum from his last point, fired a powerful jump serve!

BOOM!!!

Smack!!!

The ball blasted off Kobzar's arm, soaring high and out beyond the baseline.

Yaku sprinted out and underhanded the ball back to the net.

Mikhail leapt, adjusting for the attack!

"Double block!" Taichi and Ramos both jumped.

SMACK!!!—

Mikhail barely avoided the block, tipping a delicate sharp cross.

Oikawa covered it. Santiago jumped from the back row to set—Conte launched for a back-row attack!

"Double block!" Igor and Mikhail jumped.

BOOM!!!

The ball squeezed past the left side of the block, falling into Nizhnev's deep corner.

Smack!

Yaku saved it, though the pass was only half-accurate.

Kobzar adjusted, setting it high to the left wing. Viktor jumped to spike!

BOOM!!!

SMACK!!!

The ball ricocheted off Taichi's arm and popped up.

"One touch!"

Viktor and Taichi stared at each other over the net, neither satisfied.

"Chance ball!"

Santiago passed steadily. Oikawa slowed the tempo.

Taichi pulled back for a big approach.

"Triple block!"

Mikhail waited, stepping across just after Oikawa's hands left the ball, timing it perfectly.

—BOOM!!!

The ball tore over the block, smashing Ivan's hand in the deep corner and flying out.

A monstrous cross-court, over-the-block spike!

Point for Taichi!

[San Juan 20 – 17 Nizhnev]

"I'm sorry!" Ivan shouted.

Why couldn't he dig that?He had clearly tracked the ball's trajectory!

Ivan had never regretted neglecting his receiving practice as much as he did now. He had always believed that their towering blocks could resolve everything—and even if a ball slipped through, Yaku-san would cover it.

But…how could he always rely on others?If he thought receiving looked cool, he should've worked harder on it...

"Damn it!"

Ivan clenched his fists tightly.

"Ivan, don't chase after the ball. Keep your centre of gravity lower when receiving," Yaku said calmly, not even glancing at him. "All you need to think about is lifting the ball high enough. Leave the rest to us."

"Yaku-san, but—"

"During a match, focus only on what youcando," Yaku cut in firmly. "If you want to change who you are as a player, do it in daily training."

"Yes!!!"

Conte's second serve!

BOOM!!!

"Mine!"

Kobzar quickly bumped it up. Igor sprinted down the left sideline and jumped for the spike!

"Double block!" Taichi and Ramos both jumped.

A gap appeared between them; Ramos's position was further right, better blocking the cross shot.

But cross shots weren't Igor's forte to begin with.

"Not high enough to fully clear the block, and leaving a bait gap like that…"

Igor's arm tensed, muscles swelling as he drove the ball straight into Taichi's block!

Bruno in the back row retreated, not worried about Taichi's block being penetrated, but ready in case of a touch out.

"Now!"

The moment Taichi's arms stiffened to block, Igor suddenly pulled back, gently tapping the ball and twisting his wrist.

BANG!!

Taichi's arms couldn't retract in time.Just as Igor was about to brush the ball against his block—

Swish!

THUD!

The ball hit the floor just outside San Juan's left sideline and bounced away.

Igor's spike out!

[San Juan 21 – 17 Nizhnev]

"Why?!"

Igor's eyes widened.In his mind, it should have been a touch out.He had never underestimated Taichi, which was why he waited until Taichi's blocking form stiffened to tip it.

However, Taichi, though unable to retract his arms, pressed them down and forward at the last second, narrowly avoiding Igor's tip.

On the surface, it looked like a mutual blunder—Taichi misjudging the block direction, Igor simply missing wide.

"So strong!"

Hinata Shōyō watched from the stands, eyes sparkling with admiration.

Even with that kind of height, they had to fight so desperately at the net!

Hinata's heart surged. How fortunate he was to stand on the world stage with players like these.

"Tweet!—"

Conte's third serve!

BOOOM!!!

But it wasn't just the opponent's momentum rising.

The ball zipped between Kobzar and Viktor, landing precisely on Nizhnev's baseline and bouncing out.

"122 km/h!"

Conte's service ace!

Conte's fourth serve!

Smack!!!

Maybe he could have handled it slightly better than Igor—but on this court, there was no "better" three-man block than those three.
