During the third match, Kunimi Akira and Yahaba Shigeru were subbed out, replaced by Yamabuki Yūjin and Tōma Kaoru. On Karasuno's side, Yamaguchi Tadashi took over for Ennoshita Chikara. Despite the fierce competition, Aoba Johsai still claimed the first set.

Tsuchichi Arata walked nervously to the service line. Unlike most players, he had maxed out his intelligence stat—he recognized nearly every player on the court.

Aoba Johsai's lineup was familiar, of course, but even Karasuno's Nishinoya Yū, Tanaka Ryūnosuke, Kageyama Tobio, and Hinata Shōyō were names he had studied beforehand.

"I never thought I'd get to play against these senpais...I..." His legs trembled, a mix of excitement and anxiety surging within him.

"He serves decently in practice. Besides, it's just a scrimmage—he needs to get used to this kind of pressure."

Still, Aoba Johsai's players were worried. Normally, Yahaba and Watari—both good at calming teammates—would step in, but since neither were on the court, the others could only watch.

Tsuchichi gripped the ball tightly, his palms slick with sweat. He forced himself to breathe, trying to steady his pounding heart.

Taking a deep breath, he tossed the volleyball into the air.

"Oh? Surprisingly not bad!" Mizoguchi remarked. His toss and approach looked stable.

Bang!

Smack!

Thankfully, it was a float serve, so it hadn't been too powerful. Kyōtani caught the rebounded ball, glared at Taichi, then turned to Tsuchichi.

"At least you didn't chicken out. Work on your serve after the match."

This wasn't a game. It wasn't just practice.

This was a match.

He was put into score.
