Chapter 97

Victory?

In that decisive moment, as the ball hit the ground, the scoreboard froze, announcing the match's outcome.

The news of victory surged through the tense, electrified air like a current, yet it seemed to need a moment to truly register in everyone's minds.

The players of Aoba Johsai remained rooted in place, as if unable to believe what had just happened.

Joy, like the first rays of dawn, slowly illuminated their stunned expressions.

"We…we actually won!" Their eyes sparkled with a mix of disbelief and elation.

On the other side, Shiratorizawa's players froze in their final, desperate postures, as though time had stopped for them in that fleeting instant.

"Farewell, my paradise," Tendō Satori murmured, tilting his head back to stare at the glaring lights on the ceiling, his gaze tinged with regret. Ushijima Wakatoshi stood silently, motionless and wordless, his expression unreadable.

Time seemed suspended for a few seconds before it slowly thawed, life seeping back into the scene.

"Aaaahhhhhhh?!"

"No way!"

"How is that even possible?!"

Shiratorizawa's supporters erupted into cries of disbelief and anguished wails.

"Wooohoooo!!!"

"Oikawa-senpai! Iwaizumi-senpai!"

"The first-year who scored the most points is from my class! Taichi! Well done!" Aoba Johsai's cheering section exploded with jubilant shouts.

"Kyōtani, you were amazing!" Uncle Shimizu, seated in the stands of the Sendai City Gymnasium, watched as the young man continued to grow into a core part of the team.

"We really won…sniff…" In the audience, some of the third-years who had left the volleyball club couldn't help but tear up. They understood better than most just how precious this moment was.

Thunderous applause and cheers filled the arena, a celebration of both teams' incredible performances.

"Things are going to be absolute chaos now…" Reporters scrambled to aim their cameras at the Aoba Johsai team.

Aoba Johsai had done it—they had advanced to the National Inter-High Tournament!

On the court, the players finally snapped out of their daze. Oikawa Tōru and Iwaizumi Hajime exchanged a look before pulling each other into a tight embrace. This was a moment they had waited far too long for!

Kaedehara Taichi, though equally elated, kept his outward demeanour calm, his face betraying only a hint of his inner emotions.

"Seriously, there's still Nationals ahead....." he muttered.

Before he could finish his thought, he felt a hand clasp his shoulder. It was Kindaichi, grinning as he pulled Taichi toward the group.

Kyōtani Kentarō lingered on the outskirts, caught between the thrill of this new experience and a sense of unfamiliarity in such a moment.

"What are you standing there for? Get in here and celebrate!" Yahaba Shigeru gave Kyōtani a playful shove.

Youthful energy soon took over, and the team huddled together, revelling in the joy of their hard-fought victory.

------

"Line up!"

"Thank you for the match!" For both teams, this was perhaps the most heartfelt bow they had ever exchanged.

Shiratorizawa's players left the court in silence, their expressions sombre.

Coach Irihata's smile was warm and kind. It wasn't just about winning the match; he had glimpsed something even greater—the boundless potential of his team.

He had already held Oikawa Tōru in high regard, but after this match, he realized he had still underestimated him.

The same went for Iwaizumi's incredible receives, Kyōtani's decisive spikes, and Kindaichi's impeccable blocks.

Above all, Irihata had seen a dazzling light shining from Kaedehara Taichi—the brilliance of a 'super ace'.

Initially, Irihata had thought Taichi would need more time to mature, but the first-year's rapid growth had consistently exceeded expectations.

Maybe, just maybe, they had what it took to challenge Nationals.

"Take a quick break and freshen up. The award ceremony and interviews will start soon," he called out.

"Yes, sir!" the team replied in unison.

Taichi made his way toward the restroom, intending to splash cold water on his face. Before he could get far, Oikawa Tōru draped an arm over his shoulders.

"You don't seem all that excited, especially for a first-year rookie," Oikawa teased.

"Of course not. After all, I haven't spent five straight years losing to Ushiwaka-senpai," Taichi quipped, the playful jab perfectly timed now that they had won.

"Oh?" Oikawa's arm tightened around Taichi's neck. "I'll give youonechance to rephrase that."

"Ow. My bad! My bad!" Taichi laughed, slapping at Oikawa's arm until the setter eased up slightly.

Taking a deep breath, Taichi's expression grew serious. "I'm not overly excited because I want to beat Ushiwaka-senpai one-on-one. I'll surpass him, no matter what."

Oikawa Tōru froze. This guy…wasn't satisfied with just defeating Shiratorizawa? He even harboured such lofty ambitions?

"Taichi, you really are something."

Kaedehara Taichi blinked. "???"

"Nothing. But honestly, it would have been better if Ushiwaka had shown a bit more frustration…" Oikawa mused aloud.

"Ahem." Taichi coughed meaningfully, nodding toward the figure standing by the restroom—Ushijima Wakatoshi himself.

"What?" Oikawa was still lost in his own thoughts. "I've lost to him so many times, and it's always been agonizing. If he'd broken down and cried after losing, I'd finally feel vindicated for all those years of suffering!"

"Oikawa."

"Huh?" Oikawa finally noticed the imposing presence before him. "Ah! Ah….ha…haha! Ushiwaka, you haven't left yet?"

"Taichi," Ushijima said evenly, his expression unchanging. "Next time, I will defeat you." He turned and walked away, his tone calm but resolute.

"Not a chance, Ushiwaka!" Oikawa shouted after him. "Next time, I'll beat you even faster!"

There was no response. Ushijima didn't so much as glance back.

Taichi, however, was lost in his own musings.

Wait….Was Ushijima Wakatoshi just standing here, waiting for us to come over so he could deliver that line? Would he have waited forever if we hadn't shown up?

His thoughts continued to spiral.

Oh, and now he's gone right after saying his piece. That's kind of thrilling…no chance to retort or anything.

"Taichi!"

"Huh? What is it?" Taichi snapped back to reality.

"Never mind," Oikawa muttered, brushing it off. He was eager to return to the court and bask in the cheers of his juniors and supporters.

"And the winner of the Miyagi Prefecture Inter-High Preliminaries is….Aoba Johsai High School!"

"Congratulations to them on becoming the prefectural champions!"

"To honour the remarkable performances of both teams in this match and to wish Aoba Johsai success at the upcoming Nationals, let's give them a round of applause!"

The arena erupted with thunderous applause.

"Oikawa-san," the announcer said, stepping forward. "As the captain of Aoba Johsai, how does it feel to defeat your long-time rivals in today's final?"

"Well, it's certainly…" Oikawa's words faltered under the piercing gazes of Iwaizumi Hajime and Matsukawa Issei.

After all, this was about the school's reputation as well. "...It's incredibly gratifying. Shiratorizawa is a very strong opponent, and we're extremely happy to have won this match."

"And what are your goals for the upcoming Nationals?"

"I think that question is best answered by our ace," Oikawa replied, stepping aside and pushing Kaedehara Taichi forward.

The announcer hesitated briefly. This was the first time Aoba Johsai had officially introduced their ace in public, and it turned out to be a first-year who had joined the team less than six months ago.

Still, the number 10 jersey had left a deep impression on the announcer. The player's omnipresence during the match and his decisive finishing shot—would be etched onto their memory for years to come. He had even been the subject of yesterday's 'Hello, Miyagi' segment.

The announcer extended the microphone toward the black-haired boy, eager to hear his response.

Taichi took a step forward, his voice steady and clear. "Our goal is simple: to conquer Nationals."
