Chapter 206

The Youth National Team's Keisuke Unnan served. It was a jump serve of moderate power, targeting the back row's Hoshiumi Kōrai.

Sakusa Kiyoomi approached, took off, and swung for the spike.

"Double block!"

Bokuto Kōtarō and Kiryū Wakatsu jumped, sealing off both the straight and cross-court angles.

Keisuke had already positioned himself along the left sideline, while Ushijima Wakatoshi retreated to the baseline.

Since Sakusa's height wasn't enough to spike over the block, nor was his power sufficient to blast through it, the Youth National Team focused their defence on covering for a potential touch-out play.

Bang!

Sakusa adjusted his swing and aimed for a waist-high shot to avoid the blockers. However, stationed at the sideline was Oikawa Tōru.

Smack!

But with Oikawa forced to take the first touch, the Youth National Team lacked a setter in this rotation.

Ushijima stepped in and sent the ball back up.

He's getting faster!

—"Oikawa!"

—"Atsumu!"

[Reserve Team 25 – 24 Youth National Team]

And on the opposing side, leaving a weapon like that unguarded was simply not an option!

"Nice set, Mi-ya-se-npai!" Taichi gritted his teeth.

Not because he was frustrated at being used as a decoy—but because he realized Atsumu hadn't even aimed for a decisive score.

He just wanted to test if the play in his mind could be executed in reality.

"Right back at ya." Atsumu smirked in satisfaction.

This was his response to the last time their quick attack had been countered. The setter was the one who dictated the game.

As possession switched, Taichi stepped onto the service line.

Closing his eyes, he exhaled. When he opened them again, his mind was completely clear.

The Youth National Team's back row consisted of Tatsuki Washio, Ushijima, and Oikawa. A regular serve wouldn't faze them at all.

Then—

Taichi tossed the ball, took three quick steps, and leapt.

Right hand!

The Youth National Team locked onto his movement, making their first read.

Boom!

A powerful jump serve!

They made their second read and took a step back, preparing to receive.

—Wait!

"Tatsuki! Front!" Oikawa shouted.

The ball arced through the air, but instead of carrying deep, it curved sharply—

A short serve!

Washio, initially preparing to retreat, suddenly realized his mistake. But in that brief instant, his brain stalled.

His body, however, obeyed Oikawa's command and instinctively dove forward. His mind snapped back into action.

Smack!

Washio barely managed to pop the ball up.

Oikawa rushed in and prioritized height over precision, setting it toward the middle.

Kiryū Wakatsu took two steps and launched himself skyward.

The Reserve Team's front-row blockers were the most uncoordinated pair—Sakusa lacked raw power, and Chigaya lacked technical skill.

Boom!!!

Kiryū ruthlessly targeted Sakusa's hands!

Smack!

The ball shot through his block as if punching through paper!

"Mine!"

Hoshiumi lunged forward, keeping the ball alive with a one-handed save just before it hit the floor.

Sakusa and Hoshiumi tumbled to the ground.

With that one spike, Kiryū had simultaneously taken out two of the Reserve Team's attackers!

Atsumu immediately shifted right to cover.

"Left / Right Wing!"

Kageyama Tobio and Kaedehara Taichi both charged from the back row.

—Whoosh!

The ball soared toward the right wing, Taichi beginning his approach from the very backline.

Thud!

Taichi exploded into the air.

"Triple block!"

With the ball set high, the Youth National Team had time to form their most formidable wall.

Bokuto Kōtarō, Kiryū Wakatsu, and Washio Tatsuki aligned, sealing Taichi's path.

"Wrist rotation…"

Taichi targeted the edge of Bokuto's palm and snapped his shot.

Smack!

The ball struck Bokuto's arm, rebounded off the net, and tumbled toward the Youth National Team's court.

"Not over yet!!"

Iizuna Tsukasa dashed in from the back and kicked the ball upward before it could touch the ground!

Iizuna's eyes darted to Taichi—he was sure of what he had just seen.

That focus when aiming at the block—it was just like Sakusa's!

The Youth National Team scrambled to recover their formation. Ushijima bumped the ball up, and Oikawa leapt from the back row for a spike.

Kageyama handled the receive.

Sakusa, Hoshiumi, and Taichi all went up for the attack at once!

Atsumu jumped, his hands making a setting motion—

But at the last second, he tapped the ball over!

A quick setter dump!

"Shit—!"

The Youth National Team was caught off guard! Their focus had been entirely on the three attackers—none of them noticed Atsumu's move!

His trademark fox-like grin spread across his face.

Finishing this rally his way was the perfect conclusion to the exchange.

Smack!

A foot suddenly slid under the ball.

"Hey! Hey!! HEY!!!"

Bokuto Kōtarō, at the last possible moment, saved the point!

Kiryū scooped the ball up, and Ushijima hammered it down from the back row!

Boom!

Smack!

Hoshiumi chested the ball to keep it in play, landing hard on his back.

"Damn it! We're still going!!!"

—"Give me the ball!"

For the first time, Sakusa Kiyoomi shouted.

Kageyama immediately fed him a high-middle set.

This is incredible.

To be surrounded by so many strong players—

To defeat them all!

The strongest, of course, is ME!!!

Sakusa's arm snapped like a whip—

CRACK!

The ball spun violently as it left his fingertips.

Across the net, Taichi's eyes widened.

And landed out of bounds beyond the Youth National Team's baseline.

Thump

Sakusa Kiyoomi's spike had gone out. Point to the Youth National Team.

[Reserve Team 25 – 25 Youth National Team]

Sakusa Kiyoomi stepped up to serve—

Service Ace!

[Reserve Team 28 – 27 Youth National Team]

Oikawa Tōru adjusted his positioning and set the ball toward the middle—Ushijima Wakatoshi soared over the net, spiking straight down past the block!

[Reserve Team 28 – 28 Youth National Team]

"How long is this match going to last…?"

"They're seriously pushing the Youth National Team to their limit?"

"I mean, the second-years are already impressive, but those two first-years from Miyagi are ridiculous!!!"

Not that he minded.

Even as he stood on the court with them, he couldn't shake the feeling that he simply didn't belong among this group of monsters.

The match had reached a level where the ball seemed to blur from sheer speed. No one could predict who would score next—let alone how they would do it.

At that moment, Miya Atsumu stepped onto the service line.

"Please, just don't mess around this time…" Ojiro Aran muttered on the sideline, clasping his hands together in silent prayer.

He had long since accepted that Miya Atsumu's serving mistakes embarrassedhimmore than they did Atsumu himself.

Even though—technically—no one could see his face turning red.

Bang!

In this crucial moment, Miya Atsumu went with his most familiar serve—a deadly float serve.

Bokuto Kōtarō was forced to step in and receive—His first touch veered off the court.

Iizuna Tsukasa had already started running before the ball even left Bokuto's arms. His lack of trust in his teammates' reception was, ironically, the greatest form of trust he could give them.

Iizuna reached the ball just in time and sent it back into play—Washio Tatsuki leapt up for the attack!

Smack!

Miya Atsumu received the ball with ease.

Kageyama Tobio set the ball to the left.

Hoshiumi Korai sprinted inside the three-meter line, preparing to launch—

"Double block!"

Ushijima Wakatoshi and Keisuke Unnan jumped, sealing off his attack route.

Hoshiumi didn't spike.

The ball continued its trajectory toward the far left—where Kaedehara Taichi had already taken flight.

"Not a chance!"

[Reserve Team 29 – 28 Youth National Team]

Oikawa Tōru: "..."

"I got it!"

Oikawa quickly repositioned, sending a high set to the middle.

BOOM!!

Kaedehara Taichi and Hoshiumi Korai.

Inside the three-meter line! Let's see you stop this!!
