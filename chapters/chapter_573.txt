A two-on-one!

"Yeah," Komori Motoya responded gravely, fully agreeing.

They used to think the gap between Ryujin and other teams was mainly due to middle blockers. Now they realized that was just wishful thinking.In fact, apart from libero and setter, Ryujin clearly lagged behind in every position.

And even in those two roles, Oikawa and Kageyama weren't weaker than Miya Atsumu. Libero-wise, Yaku and Fabio were on the same level as Komori.

As Ryujin's players gradually became aware of their true position in this tournament, even always-smiling players like Komori and Atsumu found it hard to keep up their cheerful faces.
