Overhand kill!

Hinata Shōyō scored!

That kind of speed was terrifying—but—

First-touch quick attack!

BOOM!

Taichi's blocking hands suddenly pressed downward.

In that instant, Alan's spiking angle was severely compressed.

"Damn it!"

BOOM!!

Whoosh—!

"This brat…!"

This was what the elite level looked like—even with his speed, he couldn't shake them.

Whoosh!—

Kaedehara Taichi scored with a solo block!
