Smack!

If they relaxed now, they would just be giving Ryujin breathing space.

Just like Ryujin was trying to completely crush them early on, San Juan needed to cling onto them tightly, giving them no room to relax.

"Tweet!—"The service whistle blew again.

Atsumu tossed the ball high, ran up, leapt, and launched his second serve!

BOOOM!!!

"So he switched to a power serve!"

"—?!"

THUD!!!

"What was that?"

Atsumu scored another service ace!

[San Juan 4 - 11 Ryujin]
