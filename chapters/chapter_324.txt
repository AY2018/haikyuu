[Aoba Johsai 22 - 20 Karasuno]

Ennoshita Chikara stood frozen.For a split second, he had actually believed he could receive that ball.

After all, Aoba Johsai's first touch had been slightly close to the net, and Kaedehara Taichi's approach hadn't looked like a full-power attack. With Tanaka Ryūnosuke blocking and both Nishinoya Yū and Ennoshita Chikara watching the ball closely, they hadn't expected Kaedehara Taichi to strike so aggressively.

"With such a perfect opportunity, they're not going for a second attack?"

Aoba Johsai's strategy had suddenly shifted, catching Karasuno off guard. Still, this made it easier for Karasuno to organize their blockers.

Karasuno's front row shifted left in preparation. Aoba Johsai's front-row attacker, Yūki Yōji, moved aside.
