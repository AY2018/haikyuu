Chapter 416

Even so...they couldn't stop Kaedehara Taichi.

Not even once.

Thwack!

He was aiming straight for Kaedehara Taichi!

Swish!

Kamomedai's real trump card—Hoshiumi's back-row attack!

Super high reach attack!

BANG!—

That angle made a tip shot impossible!
