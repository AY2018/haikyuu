After Meian Shūgo scored with that blazing fast attack, AD had been completely pushed onto the back foot, unable to counter at all. There wasn't anything wrong with their response per se—they simply couldn't stop losing points. If onehadto find a reason, then…

"Don't get cocky just because you got invited to V1. I think your goal should be higher than just being some average pro."
