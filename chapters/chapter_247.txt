This can't be over yet!

Akaashi Keiji swung his left foot and kicked the ball high into Fukurōdani's court.

—"Bokuto!"

Both voices called out at the same time. Konoha Akinori repositioned himself and set the ball to the far-right side, where Bokuto Kōtarō soared into the air at the front row.

Kindaichi Yūtarō and Iwaizumi Hajime jumped up together.

BOOM!

A super high-contact spike from Bokuto Kōtarō!

Kaedehara Taichi did not join the block, instead retreating to cover the cross-court angle—but the ball was aimed straight down the line!

The volleyball skimmed over Iwaizumi's fingertips. Taichi, stationed along the cross-court path, and Oikawa Tōru, positioned at the straight-line boundary, froze for a split second.

Smack!

Kunimi Akira dug the ball up!!!

"Keep it alive!"

"Nice receive, Kunimi!"

Oikawa Tōru sprinted toward the far end of the court and jumped, sending a high set across the entire Aoba Johsai half to the left wing.

Another diagonal set spanning the full court!

Kaedehara Taichi sprinted along the sideline and took off.

"Triple block!!!"

Onaga Wataru, Akaashi Keiji, and Bokuto Kōtarō rose to form an iron wall.

At the same time, Komi Haruki positioned himself along the straight line, while Sarukui Yamato covered the cross-court.

At the moment of impact, Taichi's palm fully enveloped the ball, his wrist flexing down sharply.

Boom!—

It was a straight shot—but it cut through Onaga Wataru's left side and landed inside Fukurōdani's three-meter line.

"Tweet—!"

The relentless referee's whistle was always the first to react.

At long last, the gruelling"Demonic Third Day"had come to an end.

Despite their exhaustion, the Fukurōdani players stood together, bowing to Aoba Johsai in respect and expressing their gratitude to the audience.

"This match…I'm already satisfied with it."

Bokuto Kōtarō suddenly spoke, his gaze drifting toward Aoba Johsai's bench. The players who had just secured victory were exhausted, yet their faces were filled with laughter and excitement.

"I realized it too late," he admitted, "but getting to play against them in my last high school match…I'm really happy."

Washio Tatsuki gave Bokuto a light pat on the shoulder. The ace of Fukurōdani had always carried the weight of the team on his back.

"But—"

Bokuto turned to look at someone standing motionless near the court. Akaashi Keiji hadn't moved since the match ended.

Among Fukurōdani's starting lineup, five players were third-years. Akaashi had once been the junior of the team, but from tomorrow onward, he alone would bear the responsibility of leading Fukurōdani.

"…Bokuto-senpai!"

Akaashi's voice suddenly cracked, and tears streamed down his face, catching the third-years off guard.

"When you go pro, you must keep pushing forward! No—you have to push yourself even harder!"

"And next time—next time, no matter what—don't lose to Kaedehara Taichi again!!!"

…So that's what he was worried about?

Not the future of Fukurōdani, not his own burden—he was still thinking about this ridiculous owl.

"Hahaha…"

The third-years of Fukurōdani couldn't help but laugh.

"That's right, Bokuto! Next time, we'll all be cheering for you—so don't you dare lose!"

"Yeah!" Bokuto puffed out his chest. "I'll stake my name as anAceon it!"

Wait…could it be—?

Sensing Kunimi's gaze, Oikawa blinked and smirked.

"Never thought I'd see Kunimi work so hard to save a ball. Lucky for you, I have quick reflexes."

"Oikawa, is your hand okay?" Iwaizumi Hajime suddenly asked.

Everyone turned their attention to Oikawa, who had been subtly hiding one hand behind his back.

For a moment, panic flashed across Oikawa's face.

"You idiot, if you're injured, I swear I won't forgive you!" Iwaizumi's voice turned sharp.

"Pffft—hahaha!"
