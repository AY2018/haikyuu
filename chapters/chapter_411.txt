Volleyball's a sport where even if you don't play, you won't die.That simple truth applies to many things in life.

But the reason these players stand on this court—comes down to something personal they refuse to compromise on.

A hobby? A club activity? A professional dream? Everyone's relationship with volleyball may be different—or maybe the same.

Either way, finding a way to play that you won't regret—that's enough.

Seagulls fly undisturbed through strong winds and blizzards. Perhaps beyond strength, what they truly possess is a sense of freedom…and joy.
