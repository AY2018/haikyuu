Chapter 392

"What? He did that on purpose?"

It wasn't until the break between sets that Ichibayashi's players saw the scoring summary for the first set—and were stunned.All of their combined points, earned with blood, sweat, and tears, still couldn't match the number Kaedehara Taichi had scoredalone.

Coach Tamagawa didn't try to hide this fact. Even though he knew it might crush their spirits, pretending otherwise wouldn't change the truth.

The time for fearless challenge had passed. Now, it was time to confront the gap between them—and with that came the sharp, stinging pain known asdespair.

The match wasn't over.Whether they would choose to press on with hope or turn away in defeat—Coach Tamagawa wanted to see their final answer.

"Even though we're standing on the same court…it feels like the path we've walked iscompletelydifferent from Aoba Johsai's," Yasui Junichi muttered, letting out a long sigh.

"I thought you were about to say it's the difference betweenordinary peopleandgeniusesagain," Hanyū Kōmitsu said, rubbing his face hard.

"Haha…" Yasui let out a dry, helpless laugh. "Didn't someonealreadyteach us that lesson just now?"

"What should we have for dinner?" Matsuo Satoshi asked absentmindedly, his thoughts wandering far from the court. "We should replenish our nutrition properly…"

"Let's go!" Araki Kōsei reached a hand into the middle of their group. "Wasn't it for moments like this that we pushed through all the hard times?"

Shiramine Sōji shot him a surprised glance and placed his hand over Araki's. "Didn't expect something that fired up to come out ofyourmouth…"

"Add mine too!" Matsukawa Takuya pressed his palm on top.

One by one, the Ichibayashi players gathered in a circle, hands overlapping. As their bodies drew close, so did their resolve, twisting together into a single unified will.

"Let's give them a match they'llneverforget!"

"ICHIBAYASHI—!"

"LET'S GO—!!"

Their rallying cry drew the attention of the Aoba Johsai bench.

"No idea what just happened, but they still seem pretty fired up," Kunimi Akira said lazily.

"Let's do it too!" Kindaichi Yūtarō eagerly stretched out a hand.

With a look of anticipation in his eyes, Yamabuki Yūjin and Tōma Kaoru both placed their hands over his. Not long after, Nakamori Daiki and Yūki Yōji joined in without much care.

"Taichi, Kunimi!" Kindaichi's eyes sparkled as he called them.

"Nothanks.That's embarrassing," Kunimi rejected him flatly, even bothering to give a reason.

Taichi pointed to the other end of the bench. "Get the mad dog senpai to join in, andthenI'll consider it."

"Ugh…" Kindaichi hesitated but still squeaked out a tentative, "Kyōtani-senpai…?"

"Hmph.Idiot." Kyōtani Kentarō scoffed, still sulking over how he hadn't gotten to let loose in the first set.

"Alright, alright." Watari Shinji dragged Kyōtani closer. "A bit of team spirit won't kill us."

"Quit stalling," Yahaba Shigeru pushed Taichi and Kunimi into the circle.

"AOBA JOHSAI—!"

"RULE THE COURT!!!"

With a dominating win in the first set, Aoba Johsai kept their regular formation for the second. Ichibayashi, on the other hand, swapped in Araki Kōsei to go head-to-head with Kaedehara Taichi—clearly aiming to prevent a rotation lock and challenge him directly with their ace.

Ichibayashi served first.Yasui Junichi stepped up to the line.

Looking across the net, the only apparent weak point in Aoba Johsai's receiving lineup was Kindaichi Yūtarō.

Bang!

Yasui fired a jump float serve toward Aoba Johsai's front court.

Kindaichi took a step back, knees bent and centre of gravity lowered. Given how often he was targeted on serve receive, he knew full well what kind of position he was in.

There's no way I can suddenly become a receiving genius,Kindaichi thought.But I don't need a perfect pass either.

Just as Watari-senpai had taught him—by keeping some distance between himself and the ball, his view of the court expanded.

With enough space, he could react.

Smack!

Kindaichi dug the ball cleanly.

The ball arced toward the right. Yahaba swiftly moved to cover.

Though the receive had been slightly off, and Yahaba had to reposition—none of that mattered.

Whoosh—!

The ball sailed high toward the right wing. Kaedehara Taichi leapt into the air.

"Triple Block!" Araki Kōsei, Matsuo Satoshi, and Shiramine Sōji all jumped together.

BOOM!!!

No matter how tightly they formed their wall, it was useless if their hands didn't touch the ball.

Smack!

The spike slammed into Yasui Junichi's arm and flew out of bounds.

"Damn it!" They'dknownTaichi might aim for that razor-thin sideline, but that didn't mean they could stop it.

Kaedehara Taichi scored with a spike.

[Aoba Johsai 1 – 0 Ichibayashi]

Service changed hands.Aoba Johsai to serve.

Kindaichi delivered a powerful jump serve. Hanyū Kōmitsu received it smoothly.

Matsuo moved in to set. Araki approached and leapt.

"Triple Block!" Yahaba, Taichi, and Yūki jumped in unison.

BOOM!!

The ball brushed Yahaba's fingertips and slammed onto Aoba Johsai's side.

Araki Kōsei scored with a spike.

[Aoba Johsai 1 – 1 Ichibayashi]

—"Once again, Ichibayashi stays neck-and-neck with Aoba Johsai in the opening of the second set! It's an ace vs. ace showdown!"

Ichibayashi reclaimed the serve.

Shiramine Sōji's jump serve flew in. Nakamori Daiki made the receive.

Yahaba covered and tossed a high ball toward the middle.

Thud!

Kaedehara Taichi ran in and exploded into the air.

So high…!

"Triple Block!" Uchizawa Iori, Araki Kōsei, and Matsuo Satoshi jumped to meet him.

Both Ichibayashi block formations had revolved around Araki and Matsuo as the central axis, with the middles rotating in from both sides.

It was the best blocking strategy they could muster—but—

BOOM!!!

The spike sailed above the blockers and smashed right onto Ichibayashi's end line, bouncing out.

Even in set two, they still had no answer for Kaedehara Taichi.

Kaedehara Taichi scored with another spike.

[Aoba Johsai 2 – 1 Ichibayashi]

—"Taichi scores again with a powerful hit! Can Araki fire back with a response?"

Aoba Johsai to serve.

Yahaba sent over a jump float. Hanyū picked it up with ease.

Matsuo leapt for the toss. Kunimi was already in front of him, hands raised.

Perfect!Even if it was just one blocker, it was enough to disrupt them. Satisfied, Matsuo sent the ball to the middle.

Araki Kōsei approached and jumped. Like Taichi, he was the type of ace who won with height and power.

"Double Block!" Taichi and Yūki jumped up—both matching him in sheer height.

BOOM!!!—

Araki hesitated, then tried to avoid the block and spike around them.

—Smack!

Taichi pressed his block down withperfecttiming, stuffing the spike straight back into Ichibayashi's court.

Thud!

The ball hit the ground.

Kaedehara Taichi scored with a block.

[Aoba Johsai 3 – 1 Ichibayashi]

"…Your eyes waver too much," Taichi muttered, narrowing his gaze—his voice carried a hint of disappointment.

Araki clenched his fists.Was dodging the block really just an escape?Even the slightest moment of hesitation—Taichi had picked up on it instantly.

Smack!

Matsuo clapped Araki's back hard.

"The match is just getting started. As our ace, you can't afford even a second of doubt."

"Tch—" Araki straightened his back. "You didn't have to hitthathard…"

"Sorry. Won't happen again."

Aoba Johsai served again.

Yahaba's second serve flew over.

Ichibayashi stuck to the same strategy. Matsuo pulled Kunimi out of position again and sent the ball to the middle.

"Let's go again!"

Araki jumped. In front of him stood the same block—Taichi and Yūki.

BOOM!!!

Without hesitation, Araki slammed the ball right at Taichi's arms.

Smack!

The ball shot high and flew outside Aoba Johsai's end line.

"If you dare block me—then I'll make youfeelit!"

Unlike Yasui Junichi, Araki Kōsei was aiming for a spikeoff the hands!

Kaedehara Taichi lightly brushed his bangs back with his fingertips as he landed, gaze as calm and sharp as a still lake.

Nakamori Daiki effortlessly received the ball. "If this is all the distance between us, then you've still got a long way to go."

Yahaba Shigeru quickly filled in, sending a steady set toward the middle. Kaedehara Taichi leapt again. A triple block formed in front of him—Araki Kōsei at the core of it.Yet,the corner of Taichi's lips curled up into a faint, elusive smirk.

BOOM!!!

Another high-hand spike!

The ball shot cleanly past the blockers and slammed down hard into Ichibayashi's backcourt.

Thump!

The ball hit the floor. Observant spectators might have noticed—it landed inexactlythe same spot as the last spike Taichi delivered.

"Damn it!" Hanyū Kōmitsu cursed as he failed to save it with a diving receive, pounding the floor in frustration. It wasn't just the sheer height of the spike—the real issue was Kaedehara Taichi's meticulous control.That was what made him uncontainable.

Kaedehara Taichi scored again!

[Aoba Johsai 4 – 1 Ichibayashi]

The collapse in the second set came even faster than anyone in the crowd had imagined.

Even switching Araki Kōsei in to replace Yasui Junichi had made no visible difference.

But the players on Aoba Johsai didn't see it that way. They couldfeelthe shift. The Ichibayashi players on court were laser-focused now—sharpened, steady.The air around them crackled with that quiet, mounting pressure. There was no fear. No hesitation.

"Just based on that alone…you guys should already feel proud of yourselves," Yahaba murmured under his breath as he tossed the third serve.

Bang!

Unluckily—or perhaps too luckily—the ball skimmed the top of the net and dropped awkwardly toward Ichibayashi's side.

Smack!

The next to dive in was Matsuo Satoshi. He crashed into the floor just as hard—but this time, he got it.The ball floated back above Ichibayashi's court.

He drove the spike downward against the same wall that Araki had twice failed to penetrate. His eyes gleamed with determination.

Bang!

Smack!

The ball crashed into Yūki Yōji's arms—

And bounced back up into Ichibayashi's side again!

A rebound!

Matsuo leapt at the net for the attack.

Araki Kōsei went up for the spike!

"Double block!"

Araki Kōsei scored with a powerful spike!
