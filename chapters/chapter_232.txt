Chapter 232

Irihata Nobuteru stood on the sidelines, his expression a mixture of excitement and concern.

Just moments ago, team manager Mizoguchi Sadayuki had brought news about their next opponent—the second representative from Tokyo, Fukurodani Academy. Without a doubt, this was another powerhouse team.

In fact, by the time teams reached the quarterfinals of the national tournament, every opponent had been battle-tested. There was no such thing as an easy match at this stage.

Kaedehara Taichi was in peak form. His relentless scoring streak on the national stage had already set a record worth paying attention to. The reactions of both the commentators and the spectators made that clear.

What worried Irihata was whether Taichi could sustain this level of intensity without exhausting himself before the afternoon match. However, forcing him to slow down now seemed equally unreasonable.

"Sigh…Sometimes, having a player this good is a real headache..." Irihata muttered.

Mizoguchi shot him a look of admiration. Despite his supposedly troubled tone, the coach's grin was practically stretching to his ears.

Taichi nodded seriously.

He suddenly remembered—

In the original Spring High, during the quarterfinals—

Hinata Shōyō's fever.

Iizuna Tsukasa's injury.

…He needed to be more careful.

Coach Irihata said nothing more.

Kaedehara Taichi glanced around the court—

And finally noticed—

Bokuto Kōtarō and Akaashi Keiji.

They were watching him.

Kaedehara Taichi met their gaze.

He gave a slight nod.

So—

They were the next opponents.

Finally.

The match he had been waiting for.
