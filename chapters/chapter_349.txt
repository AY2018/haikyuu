Chapter 349

Kyōtani Kentarō never had a clear-cut career plan like Bokuto Kōtarō did, but when it came to hislovefor volleyball, he would never lose to anyone.

After clashing with his upperclassmen and being ostracised, Kyōtani was forced to leave his team. From that moment on, he had always been alone in the world of volleyball—like a lone star burning in silence.

No team.

No teammates.

No goal.

Even so, thestray Mad Dognever stopped chasing volleyball.

Every jump, every spike—the moment the ball made contact with his palm—it was the purest form of his passion for the sport, igniting deep within him.

Without a team, he drifted from gym to gym, looking for matches where they needed an extra player.

Without teammates, he pushed himself to the limit, training alone.

Without a goal…

Kyōtani Kentarō could only release his love for volleyball on the court, as if the entire world had narrowed down to just him and the bouncing ball.

Until one year ago, during Golden Week—when Oikawa Tōru and Kaedehara Taichi pushed open the doors of the town gymnasium.

Dominate the nation!

This goal, which had left countless high school players with bitter regrets, was finally achieved by Aoba Johsai. And Kyōtani Kentarō had been one of them.

The joy of that moment was something he wouldneverforget.

After that, Oikawa Tōru went to Argentina, and Kaedehara Taichi began preparing for his professional career.

And Kyōtani Kentarō finally saw the path he wanted to take.

Only volleyball!

Even at eighty years old, he wanted to remain involved in the sport in some way.

Bang!

BANG!!!

Bokuto Kōtarō fired a straight-line shot past the blockers, the ball zipping past Kindaichi's feet and out of reach.
